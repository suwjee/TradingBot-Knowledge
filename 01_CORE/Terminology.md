# Terminology

Behaviors: A, S, E, StopAll only. Calculation/evidence: Reaction, Reset, Internal Reaction, Blue Line, physical Order, OrderAudit. Reaction Mode A/B is native detector mode, unrelated to behavior A or Order_A/B/C. Order_A, Order_B, and Order_C name independently proved physical-Order creation causes. A single Order identity is (FirstIndex,BreakIndex) and may have several causes.

Red and Blue are invariant S/E families. E1, E2, ... are recursive number/state, not separate algorithms. Source index/time, decision index/time, exact event time, and presentation index are distinct. A Reset's exact secondTime, when present, takes precedence over its main display time.
